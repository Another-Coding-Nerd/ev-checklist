# First 24 Hours

- [ ] Ask your delivery specialist to confirm the car is marked DELIVERED
      in Tesla's system before you leave — there are reports of the phone
      app refusing login the next day when this step gets missed.
- [ ] Verify the car shows as delivered in your phone app and that all app
      features work.
- [ ] **Use your free trials before they expire** — new cars may include a
      free month of Premium Connectivity and a free FSD trial period.
      Both are use-it-or-lose-it; the car will notify you on screen when
      each is about to expire. Evaluate them while you have them so you
      can decide whether to subscribe.
- [ ] Confirm you have paper copies of all purchase paperwork, including
      the signed purchase agreement — get paper even if told you can pull
      documents electronically later.
- [ ] Visit and check the local Supercharger while you're still near the
      delivery center, if you haven't already.
- [ ] When your registration arrives, confirm it lists the correct
      trim/configuration (AWD/RWD, Standard/Premium/Performance, etc.) —
      a mismatch can affect insurance coverage and resale value later.
- [ ] Before leaving, have the delivery rep demonstrate: how to charge
      (opening the charge port, plugging in), and the correct frunk-
      closing technique (closing it wrong can cause misalignment).
- [ ] **Locate all five manual releases before you need them** — none
      require power:
      - Front doors: manual release handle above the window switches
        (pull to open mechanically; avoid unless necessary — the power
        opener vents the window first to prevent trim damage).
      - Rear doors: small pull tab tucked into the door panel near the
        latch (may need a flashlight to find it).
      - Trunk interior: glow-in-the-dark button on the left inner wall
        of the cargo area — push to release if trapped.
      - Frunk interior: internal release button (glows in the dark).
      - Stuck charge cable: pull cable inside the trunk directly behind
        the charge port to mechanically unlatch. **Stop charging first
        before using this release** (tap Stop in the app or on the
        screen) — Tesla warns that pulling the manual release while
        actively charging risks electrocution.
- [ ] **If you have a garage, set a location-based trunk height limit**
      — park in your spot, manually stop the trunk at the right height
      to clear the ceiling, then save that GPS location. The car will
      remember it and auto-limit opening height there from then on
      (Controls → Trunk).
- [ ] **Set up driver profiles if multiple people share the car** —
      each driver pairs their phone key to their own profile; the car
      loads the right seat/mirror/climate settings automatically based
      on which phone approaches. To add a driver without a physical key
      handoff: Tesla app > Security & Drivers > Add Driver — sends them
      a link to download the app and pair their own phone key.
- [ ] **Enable headlights after exit** (Controls > Lights > Headlights After
      Exit) — keeps the headlights on for a short time after you park and
      walk away, lighting your path. Easy to overlook and genuinely useful
      at night.
- [ ] **Enable adaptive headlights** (Controls > Lights > Adaptive Headlights)
      — automatically dims the headlights on the side of oncoming traffic
      so you don't blind other drivers while keeping your side of the road
      fully lit. Worth turning on and leaving on.
- [ ] **Set forward collision warning to Early** (Controls > Autopilot >
      Forward Collision Warning) — gives you the maximum reaction time if
      the car detects a potential collision ahead. No reason to leave it on
      the default late setting.
- [ ] **Set speed limit warning to Display** (Controls > Autopilot > Speed
      Limit Warning) — shows the current speed limit on screen at all times.
      No chime, just a persistent visual reference.
- [ ] **Turn on "Apply brakes when regen is limited"** (Controls > Dynamics)
      — when the battery is cold or near full, regenerative braking weakens
      and the car can feel like it has no engine braking. This setting
      applies friction brakes to compensate, keeping deceleration consistent.
      Especially useful in winter.
- [ ] **Enable Joe Mode** (Controls > Safety > Joe Mode) — reduces the
      volume of in-cabin chimes and alerts without disabling them. Named
      after a Tesla owner whose kids were tired of all the sounds. Good
      default for most drivers.
- [ ] **Confirm Walk-Away Door Lock is on** (Controls > Locks > Walk-Away
      Door Lock) — if this is off, the car will not auto-lock when you
      walk away with your phone, even though it looks like it should.
      Easy setting to miss; real security gap if it's left off.
- [ ] **Consider enabling PIN to Drive** (Controls > Safety > PIN to
      Drive) — requires a PIN on the touchscreen before the car can be
      driven, even with a valid key card or phone key. One-time setup;
      protects against a stolen key or phone being enough to take the
      car.
- [ ] **Enable Phone Left Behind alert** (Controls > Locks) — if you
      exit the car with your phone still on the wireless charger, the car
      chirps from outside before you get too far. Worth having on: your
      phone is also your key.
- [ ] **Enable Close Windows on Lock** (Controls > Locks) — any open
      windows roll up automatically when the car locks as you walk away.
      Easy to forget you had them cracked.
- [ ] **Enable Unlock on Park** (Controls > Locks) — doors automatically
      unlock when you shift into Park so you don't have to fumble with the
      door after pulling in.
- [ ] **Enable Left Open Notifications** (Controls > Locks) — alerts you
      via the app if a door or window is still open after you walk away.
      Useful in parking lots when you're distracted.
- [ ] **Set up at least one non-Tesla charging app before your first
      road trip** — Superchargers cover most routes, but you may need
      Electrify America, EVgo, or ChargePoint as a backup. Download the
      app, add a payment method, and enable plug-in charge / autocharge
      while you're home — not in a parking lot somewhere unfamiliar
      when you actually need a charge.
- [ ] **Turn on "Only add Superchargers with Tesla pricing"** (Controls
      > Navigation > Trip Planning) — prevents the Trip Planner from
      routing you to third-party-operated Tesla chargers that use
      time-based or otherwise non-standard rates instead of Tesla's
      per-kWh pricing. Easy to miss and potentially expensive if left
      off.
- [ ] **Enable Sentry live camera view while you're in the car**
      (Controls > Safety > View Camera via Mobile App) — this must be
      toggled on physically in the car before you can use it from the
      app remotely. Requires premium connectivity. Can't be turned on
      from the app if you're already away from the vehicle.
- [ ] **Enable Sentry camera-based detection** (Controls > Safety >
      Sentry Mode > Camera-Based Detection) — allows Sentry to detect
      motion and activity around the car and save clips automatically,
      rather than only recording continuously. More useful footage, less
      noise.
- [ ] **Set Sentry Mode exclusions for home and work** (Controls > Safety
      > Sentry Mode > Exclude) — Sentry keeps the car awake and drains
      the battery. Add your home and regular work location as excluded
      zones so it only runs where you actually need it.
- [ ] **Turn on Encrypt Dashcam Recordings** (Controls > Safety) —
      recordings saved to the USB drive in the glovebox can only be
      played back inside your car or when logged into your Tesla account.
      Protects footage if the drive is stolen. To decrypt and view clips
      on a computer: plug the drive in, go to dashcam.tesla.com, and sign
      in with your Tesla account — decryption runs locally in the browser,
      nothing is uploaded to Tesla.
- [ ] **Enable "On Honk" in dashcam settings** (tap the dashcam icon >
      Settings) — pressing the horn saves the previous 10 minutes of
      footage. Useful when you witness something on the road and want to
      capture it without waiting to manually tap the icon. Can run
      alongside Auto or Manual mode.
- [ ] **Know that dashcam uses rolling overwrite** — the drive has a fixed
      buffer; older unsaved footage is continuously replaced by newer
      footage. If something happens (fender bender, road incident), save
      the clip immediately while it's still safe to do so — don't assume
      it'll still be there an hour later.
- [ ] **Enable "Reduce fan speed during calls"** (Controls > Bluetooth) —
      lowers HVAC fan volume automatically when you're on a phone call.
      The cabin fan at high speed can make calls nearly unintelligible;
      this fixes it without you having to manually turn the fan down.
- [ ] **Enable "Hey Grok" voice activation** (Controls > Audio > Voice
      Commands) — allows hands-free access to Tesla's AI assistant by
      voice. If you plan to use Grok for navigation, messages, or
      questions while driving, enable this so you don't have to tap the
      screen.
- [ ] **Set software update preference to "Advanced"** (Controls >
      Software > Software Update Preference) — opts you into updates
      sooner than the default rollout cadence. Results vary by account,
      but many owners report getting updates meaningfully earlier.
- [ ] **Adjust Cabin Overheat Protection seasonally** (Controls > Safety >
      Cabin Overheat Protection) — in summer, leave it on full (runs AC to
      keep the cabin under your set temperature). In winter, switch to
      "No AC" — it still protects the cabin but won't run the compressor
      unnecessarily, saving battery.
