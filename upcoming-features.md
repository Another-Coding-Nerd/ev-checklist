---

## Two things to keep straight

1. **Robotaxi / "unsupervised FSD" ≠ your car becomes self-driving.** Those run on specific
   hardware, in Tesla-operated, geofenced fleets. None of it retires the **"you're always the
   driver, stay alert"** rule on a supervised car.
2. **A newer FSD version does NOT change the supervision requirement.** Smoother software is
   *easier* to over-trust. A software update does not change the legal or practical obligation
   to remain alert and in control.

**Status legend**

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ✅ **Shipped** | In customer cars now (on the relevant hardware) |
| 🔄 **Rolling out** | Actively deploying in waves; not everyone has it yet |
| 📣 **Announced** | Official, dated, not yet shipped |
| 🔮 **Watch** | Stated/promised, but Tesla has a history of slipping this class of date — treat the date as aspirational |
| ⚠️ **Verify** | Sources conflict — unresolved |

## Tesla's timing track record (the yardstick for every 🔮 above)

Tesla/Musk have a long, **documented** record of announcing full-autonomy milestones years before they
ship — or at all. Read every 🔮 date above through this base rate.

| Promised (when made) | The claim | What actually happened |
|---|---|---|
| 2016–2017 | Autonomous drive **LA→NY by end of 2017**, "no controls touched" (TED, Apr 2017) | Never happened |
| Apr 2019 | **1M robotaxis on the road by 2020**; FSD "feature complete" by end of 2019 | No robotaxis in 2020; not feature-complete |
| 2020 | "Level 5 autonomy… complete this year" | Did not happen |
| 2024 | Robotaxi (Cybercab) unveil | Unveiled; **no public service** |
| Jun 2025 | Unsupervised FSD / robotaxi service "by June 2025" | Limited **geofenced** launch only |
| Jan 2026 | Walked it back: needs **~10B miles** of data before safe unsupervised deployment | Goalpost moved |
| Q1 2026 earnings | Unsupervised **consumer** FSD "**Q4 2026 at the earliest**" | The Tier 3 promise now on the clock |
| May 2026 | Unsupervised FSD "widespread in US by year-end" — *again* | TBD |

Sources: [TechCrunch (2024 promises recap)](https://techcrunch.com/2024/12/31/elon-musks-promises-for-tesla-in-2024-from-robotaxis-to-optimus-bots) · [InsideEVs (2025 targets missed)](https://insideevs.com/news/783157/musk-promises-2025-eoy-robotaxis/) · [electrek (Apr 2026 — Q4 delay "again")](https://electrek.co/2026/04/22/tesla-elon-musk-unsupervised-fsd-consumer-cars-q4-delay-again/) · [electrek (May 2026 — "by year-end, again")](https://electrek.co/2026/05/18/musk-unsupervised-fsd-widespread-us-end-of-year-smart-mobility-summit/)

**Takeaway:** a Tesla "Q4 2026 / by year-end" promise reads as **"directionally real, probably later,
likely narrower (geofenced / HW-gated)."** Don't plan around it. For this project it doesn't matter
much — the features that matter for everyday ownership are already shipped and standard. Nothing
in the 🔮 tier changes the current ownership experience.

### Note — the two camera systems vs. the NHTSA visibility probe (important)

These are two separate hardware changes. Don't conflate them.

**1. Front camera: sealed housing + washer/air cleaning (✅ confirmed for Model Y)**
- In Juniper Model Y production since ~Jan 2026. Front-bumper camera gets a sealed (anti-fog/off-gassing) housing + washer-fluid + air-burst cleaning on detected occlusion.
- This is *physical* hardware, not a compute upgrade — HW4 doesn't gate it, build date does.
- Directly addresses the **NHTSA EA26002** visibility failure mode (FSD losing the lead car in glare/fog/dust). Genuine mitigation.
- A mid-Dec 2026 build should have it — verify on the actual car before accepting delivery.

**2. Side-repeater camera: passive airflow pod (⚠️ Cybertruck only, unconfirmed for Model Y)**
- The Sep 8, 2026 Cybertruck running change: sculpted ridges + fin deflect water from the side-camera lens passively (no pump). Confirmed only on Cybertruck. 
- No announcement or source confirms this has moved to the consumer Model Y. The robotaxi Model Y has active side washers, but that's a different solution on a different vehicle.
- Monitor: if/when it ships to Model Y, it would also help with EA26002 failure modes on the side cameras.

**On the NHTSA probe itself:**
- NHTSA EA26002 covers 3.2M vehicles (open as of Oct 2026): FSD losing the lead vehicle in degraded visibility (glare/fog/dust).
- The front camera fix is real, shipped mitigation. The side-repeater fix is not yet on Model Y.
- Neither changes the "stay alert, you're always the driver" rule — that's structural to Level 2, not fixable by hardware.
